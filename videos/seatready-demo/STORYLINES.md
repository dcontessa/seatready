# Storylines considered

## Selected — One enquiry, all the way through
Follow a three-person beginner enquiry through the actual app. Begin with the training provider’s question, move into the course match, and end with a verified sandbox booking. The same three seats and USD 447 quote connect every scene. Real screen recordings carry the proof; short editorial labels explain the boundaries. Forest green, white, and pale sage from SeatReady. Local narration with no music.

## Alternative — The booking record first
Start on a completed sandbox record, then retrace each decision that produced it. Reverse chronology with close-ups of evidence fields. This depends on checkout being verified before any opener can be used.

## Alternative — AI advice, human decision
A split view distinguishes what the model proposes from what the operator and server control. The panels converge on a booking. Rejected as less direct evidence of the end-to-end experience.
